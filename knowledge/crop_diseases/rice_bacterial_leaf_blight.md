# Rice — Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB)

**Pathogen:** *Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzae* (bacterium)
**Crop:** Rice (paddy)

> Starter sample document. Replace with the authoritative ICAR-IIRR / State
> Agricultural University bulletin for your region before relying on it.

## Field symptoms

- Water-soaked lesions begin at leaf tips and margins, then turn yellow to
  straw-coloured with wavy edges.
- Lesions spread down the leaf; in severe cases whole leaves dry out.
- In the seedling stage ("kresek"), leaves wilt and roll — often mistaken for
  drought stress.
- Milky bacterial ooze may be seen on cut lesions held in water (early morning).

## Favourable conditions

- Temperature 25–34 °C with high humidity.
- Heavy rain, flooding, and strong winds that cause leaf injury.
- Excess nitrogen fertilizer and dense planting.

## Management

**Cultural**
- Use resistant/tolerant varieties recommended for your state.
- Balanced nutrition — avoid excess nitrogen; split N application.
- Good drainage; avoid deep stagnant water during outbreaks.
- Field sanitation: remove infected stubble and weed hosts.

**Chemical (only per registered label and local recommendation)**
- Copper-based bactericides or recommended antibiotics may be advised by the
  state agriculture department during outbreaks. Always confirm the currently
  registered product, dose, and pre-harvest interval with your KVK.

## When to escalate

If lesions spread rapidly across the field after rain, or seedlings show kresek
wilting, contact the local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or agriculture officer
promptly for a field diagnosis.
