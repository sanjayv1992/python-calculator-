# Paddy — Managing Lodging

> Starter sample document. PGR use must follow the registered label and local
> recommendation; verify with the KVK.

## Why paddy lodges
- Excess nitrogen and dense planting create tall, weak stems.
- Heavy rain and wind then flatten the crop, cutting yield and grain quality.

## Management (agronomy first)
- Balanced nutrition; avoid excess nitrogen; split N application.
- Ensure adequate potassium for stem strength.
- Use lodging-tolerant varieties and recommended spacing.

## PGR note
Any plant growth regulator must be used only if registered for this use, strictly
at the label dose and timing. Confirm with the local KVK.
