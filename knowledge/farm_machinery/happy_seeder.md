# Happy Seeder for Wheat after Paddy

> Starter sample document. Verify operation and subsidy details with the KVK and
> the SMAM portal.

## What it does
- Sows wheat directly into paddy residue, so farmers avoid burning stubble.
- Saves a field operation, conserves soil moisture, and builds soil health.

## Good practice
- Spread combine residue evenly before sowing.
- Calibrate seed and fertilizer rates; maintain correct PTO speed.

Machinery subsidy may be available under SMAM / Custom Hiring Centres — check the
official agrimachinery portal and your agriculture department.
