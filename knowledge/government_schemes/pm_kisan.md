# PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)

> Starter sample document. Always verify current benefits, eligibility, and
> process against the official PM-KISAN portal and latest government
> notification before advising a farmer.

## What it is

A central government income-support scheme for landholding farmer families,
providing financial benefit in three equal instalments per year, transferred
directly to bank accounts (DBT).

## Eligibility (indicative)

- Landholding farmer families whose names appear in land records.
- Subject to exclusion categories (e.g. income-tax payers, institutional
  landholders, and certain government employees/pensioners) as defined in the
  scheme guidelines.

## Documents typically required

- Aadhaar number
- Bank account details (for DBT)
- Land records / ownership details as per state process

## How to apply

- Register at the official PM-KISAN portal or through the local Common Service
  Centre (CSC) / village revenue or agriculture officer.
- Complete e-KYC as required.
- Track application and instalment status on the portal.

## AgroManch note

The Government Scheme Assistant should always cite the official guideline
document and tell the farmer to confirm the latest instalment amount and
eligibility rules on the official portal, since these are periodically revised.
