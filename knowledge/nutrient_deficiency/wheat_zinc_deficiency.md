# Wheat — Zinc Deficiency

> Starter sample document. Replace with your State Agricultural University's
> soil-test-based recommendation before relying on it.

## Field symptoms
- Interveinal chlorosis (yellowing) on middle leaves; brownish patches in severe cases.
- Stunted plants, reduced tillering, delayed maturity.

## Favourable conditions
- Sandy / light-textured, high-pH (alkaline) soils.
- Intensive rice-wheat cropping without micronutrient replenishment.

## Correction
- **Soil:** apply zinc sulphate at sowing per soil test.
- **Foliar:** spray zinc sulphate with lime to correct visible deficiency quickly.

## AgroManch note
Confirm the exact zinc sulphate rate and foliar concentration with the local KVK
and the Soil Health Card.
