# Wheat — Nutrient Management (sample guide)

> Starter sample document. Replace with your State Agricultural University's
> soil-test-based recommendation. Actual doses depend on soil test values,
> variety, irrigation, and region.

## General approach

- Base fertilizer doses on a **soil health card / soil test** wherever possible.
- Apply the full phosphorus and potassium, and part of the nitrogen, as a
  **basal** dose at sowing.
- Apply remaining nitrogen as **top dressing**, typically split at the crown
  root initiation (CRI, ~21 days) and later tillering stages, with irrigation.

## Indicative nutrient roles

- **Nitrogen (N):** vegetative growth, tillering, green colour. Deficiency shows
  as pale/yellow older leaves; excess raises lodging and disease risk.
- **Phosphorus (P):** root development and early establishment. Deficiency
  shows as poor tillering and purplish older leaves.
- **Potassium (K):** water regulation, stem strength, disease tolerance.

## Application tips

- Do not place high rates of urea in direct contact with seed.
- Ensure adequate soil moisture before top dressing nitrogen.
- Correct micronutrient deficiencies (e.g. zinc) as per soil test.
